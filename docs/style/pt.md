# Style guide: European Portuguese, `pt` (metadata only)

<!-- Appended to core.md in the system prompt. Overrides core.md where they conflict.
Portuguese is the SOURCE language of the article texts, which are shown as printed (1921/1940 orthography). This guide
covers only the metadata written for the digital edition — abstracts, chapter titles and summaries, person and place
pages and notes, chronology — which are written in English and translated back into Portuguese. -->

## 1. Standard and spelling
- **European Portuguese**, current orthography (Acordo Ortográfico de 1990, as used by Lusa and the Portuguese
  press): *ação*, *direção*, *ótimo*, months and weekdays in lower case (*julho*, *segunda-feira*).
- Never Brazilian forms: *registo* (not *registro*), *facto* (not *fato*), *equipa*, *comboio*, *autocarro*;
  *está a construir* (not *está construindo*); no *você*.
- Quotations from the source keep their 1921/1940 spelling inside «…»; everything else is modern.

## 2. Register and tone
- Clear, sober prose of a modern Portuguese reference work (the register of a good encyclopedia or a museum text).
  No *juridiquês*, no heavy nominal chains.
- Narrative past: **pretérito perfeito** (*foi fundada*, *governou*). Present only for what is still true.
- The edition's own voice is impersonal (*refere-se*, *descreve-se*), not "nós".

## 3. Names — restore the Portuguese original (most important rule)
The English metadata uses English forms and adds meaning glosses for foreign readers. In Portuguese, **every name
goes back to its Portuguese original form**, and the glosses are dropped:
- exonyms back to Portuguese: Lisbon → **Lisboa**, the Azores → **os Açores**, Oporto → **Porto**, the Canaries →
  **as Canárias**, Seville → **Sevilha**, London → **Londres**;
- historical figures in their Portuguese form: Henry the Navigator → **Infante D. Henrique**, King Manuel I →
  **D. Manuel I**, the Marquis of Pombal → **Marquês de Pombal**, Philip II of Spain → **Filipe II de Espanha (Filipe I
  de Portugal)**;
- religious dedications, chapels, churches, feasts in Portuguese: the chapel of Our Lady of Pity → **Capela de Nossa
  Senhora da Piedade**, Saint Vincent → **São Vicente**;
- titles of works and periodicals in Portuguese as published: *Longing for the Homeland* → ***Saudades da Terra***;
- **drop** meaning glosses and translations in parentheses that exist only for foreign readers: "Curral das Freiras
  (‘the nuns’ pen’)" → **Curral das Freiras**; "the Câmara Municipal (town council)" → **a Câmara Municipal**;
- Madeiran toponyms in their modern official spelling: *Câmara de Lobos*, *Porto Moniz*, *Santana*, *Fajã da Ovelha*.
  Personal names as printed in the encyclopedia's own headings (*Gaspar Frutuoso*, *João Gonçalves Zarco*).
- Honorifics as in Portuguese: *D.* (Dom/Dona), *Dr.*, *Padre*, *Frei*, *Cónego*, *Conde de…*.

## 4. Terms
- Madeiran and Portuguese terms are ordinary Portuguese words here: **freguesia, concelho, sítio, levada, fajã,
  achada, lombo, poio, palheiro, morgado, capitão-donatário, Câmara Municipal** — no italics, no glosses.
- Historical institutions and offices by their Portuguese names (*Junta Geral*, *Provedor da Fazenda*,
  *Misericórdia*).

## 5. Punctuation and typography
- Quotation marks: **« »** (aspas angulares) for primary quotations, “ ” inside them.
- Dash: spaced en dash ( – ). Ranges: 1834–1836.
- Titles of works and periodicals in italics (*Saudades da Terra*, *Diário de Notícias*).

## 6. Numbers, dates, money
- Thousands separated by a space from five digits (12 500; 1 436 305); decimal comma (756,2). Years never grouped.
- Dates: **9 de julho de 1891**; *em 1640*, *cerca de 1640*; centuries **século XVI**.
- Ordinals: 1.º, 2.ª; regnal numbers D. João I, Filipe II.
- Money as in Portugal: *réis*, *mil-réis*, *contos de réis*, *escudos*, *centavos* (no glosses). Order: "20 000
  réis", "4,20 escudos".
- Units: 12 km, 648 500 kg, 8 °C (space before the symbol); percentages 58,8 %.

## 7. Examples
- EN: "Parish in the municipality of Calheta, on the south-west coast; its levadas (irrigation channels) include the
  Levada Nova." → PT: "Freguesia do concelho da Calheta, na costa sudoeste; entre as suas levadas conta-se a Levada
  Nova."
- EN: "Governor of Madeira (1834–36), noted for reorganising the customs service after the Liberal victory." → PT:
  "Governador da Madeira (1834–1836), lembrado por ter reorganizado a alfândega após a vitória liberal."
- EN: "Chapel of Our Lady of Pity (Nossa Senhora da Piedade) at Caniçal, rebuilt in 1664." → PT: "Capela de Nossa
  Senhora da Piedade, no Caniçal, reconstruída em 1664."
- EN: "Henry the Navigator granted the captaincy of Funchal to Zarco in 1450." → PT: "O Infante D. Henrique doou a
  capitania do Funchal a Zarco em 1440."
